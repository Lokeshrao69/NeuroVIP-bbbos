How to read these results

With 20 samples and each disease from a different study, perfect scores mostly show that groups are easy to separate, not that a diagnostic signature is validated. Leave-one-disease-out models ranked samples perfectly (ROC-AUC 1.0) but the decision threshold did not transfer between studies, so accuracy on held-out stroke and glioblastoma was 0.5. Candidate genes were pre-selected from DEGs computed on all samples, which can inflate scores.
