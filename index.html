How to read the volcano plot
  • Each dot is one gene. Left–right is the size of the change (log2 fold change): right = higher in disease, left = lower.
  • Up–down is the statistical confidence (−log10 of the adjusted p-value): higher dots are more certain.
  • Red = up-regulated, blue = down-regulated; grey dots are significant but smaller than the chosen minimum change.
  • Genes in the top corners are the most important: a large change and high confidence. Hover over a dot for details.

How the pathway analysis compares with the paper
  • The paper ran KOBAS 2.0 against KEGG, PANTHER and Reactome on genes "commonly regulated in all four meningitis microvessel samples compared to all the healthy ones (p < 0.05, log2 fold change > 0.585)".
  • Here the same selection is made on the paper's count matrix: DESeq2 p < 0.05, |log2FC| ≥ 0.585, and every infected sample at least 1.5-fold from the healthy mean in the same direction. The NeuroVIP and classic runs have no per-sample data, so their selection is padj ≤ 0.05 and |FC| ≥ 1.5.
  • Over-representation: hypergeometric test against all genes tested, BH-corrected within each database, as in KOBAS. Direction: binomial test of whether the selected genes in a pathway lean up or down more than all selected genes.
  • Gene sets are the current official releases (KEGG REST, PANTHER 3.6.8, Reactome lowest-level mouse pathways), downloaded by the script. The paper used the 2019 releases, so pathway membership, names and rankings differ somewhat.
  • ★ marks pathways on the x-axis of the paper's Fig. 6e (PANTHER) or 6f (KEGG); bold names are also named in the Results text or marked with an arrow. Those labels were read from the published figure. The Reactome results (Supp. Fig. 9) were not available for comparison.
  • The "Literature" panels are BBB gene groups curated for this dashboard (gene lists in the script). They are not part of the paper.

Machine learning

Exploratory classifiers trained to distinguish disease samples from controls using gene expression.

Interpret with caution

There are only 20 samples, and each disease comes from a different study, so these results are exploratory. Models trained on two diseases and tested on the third ranked samples correctly (ROC-AUC 1.0), but their decision threshold did not carry over between studies, giving 0.5 accuracy on held-out stroke and glioblastoma. Perfect scores elsewhere mainly show that the groups are easy to separate, not that a diagnostic signature has been validated.

Help and methods

What the data are, how they were processed, and how to read each page.

About NeuroVIP

The blood–brain barrier (BBB) is formed by the endothelial cells of brain blood vessels, supported by pericytes, astrocytes and microglia (the neurovascular unit). Many CNS diseases damage the BBB. NeuroVIP asks whether diseases with very different causes (ischemic, infectious and neoplastic) produce a common gene-expression response in brain microvessels, which could point to shared therapeutic targets for protecting the barrier.

Key terms

Gene expression
How much RNA a gene produces. RNA sequencing (RNA-seq) measures it by counting sequenced RNA fragments (reads) from each gene.
Significant gene (DEG)
A differentially expressed gene: one whose expression differs reliably between disease and control animals (adjusted p-value < 0.05).
log2 fold change (log2FC)
Size of the change on a log2 scale. +1 = twice as much in disease, +2 = four times, −1 = half, 0 = no change.
Up-regulated
log2FC > 0: the gene is more active in disease (shown in red).
Down-regulated
log2FC < 0: the gene is less active in disease (shown in blue).
Adjusted p-value (padj)
Confidence that a change is real, corrected for testing ~20,000 genes at once (Benjamini–Hochberg). Smaller = more confident.
Shared gene
A gene significant in all three diseases.

Data processing

Raw reads (NCBI SRA, 20 samples)
  → FastQC 0.11.9                     quality control
  → Trim Galore 0.6.7 / Cutadapt 5.2  adapter and quality trimming (Q20, ≥ 30 bp)
  → STAR 2.7.10a                      alignment to mouse genome GRCm39 (Ensembl 115), unique reads
  → featureCounts (Subread 2.0.3)     reads per gene
  → DESeq2 1.50.2                     disease vs control within each study, padj < 0.05

Limitations

  • Each disease comes from a different study, so differences between diseases may partly reflect laboratory or batch effects. Each disease is therefore compared only with its own controls.
  • Group sizes are small (3–4 animals).
  • Microvessel preparations contain some non-endothelial cells, so not every change is endothelial-specific.
